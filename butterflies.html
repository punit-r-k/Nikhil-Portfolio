About the Music

Butterflies is a chamber piece that explores the interplay between cello and piano through delicate textures, gentle harmonic shifts, and a sense of quiet motion. The music unfolds with a balance of lyrical lines and light rhythmic energy, evoking the fluttering grace of its title.

The work highlights the cello’s warm, singing tone alongside the piano’s sensitively articulated accompaniment. Quiet reveries give way to moments of expressive intensity, drawing listeners into a landscape that is both introspective and buoyant.
