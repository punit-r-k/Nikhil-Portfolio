Biography

Short bio

A musical life in progress.

Nikhil Murali is an aspiring film and concert composer from Katy, Texas. He plays tabla and cello, and he loves to conduct. His perspective brings together the discipline of instrumental study with an enduring interest in the expressive possibilities of composition.

He studied cello under Dr. Yuliya Kim and Dr. Daniel Saenz. He studied composition under James Tabata and David Michael Hyde.
